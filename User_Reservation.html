Cooperative Rental Policies

Standard operating procedures and compliance rules for all equipment rentals

Member Terms v2.4
1

Return Inspection & Staging

All implements must be returned clean of heavy soil and debris to their designated yard stall. Complete the sign-in kiosk QR scan upon drop-off.

2

24-Hour Checkout Grace Window

Pickups must occur within 2 hours of reservation start time. Unclaimed units are released back to general pool after 24 hours without dispatch notice.

3

Fuel Replacement & Fluid Top-off

Tractors and diesel pumps depart full. Return with full tank (Off-road #2 Diesel or 87 Unleaded) or pay standard depot rate plus a 15% surcharge.
